About Alertify

Alertify is a high-availability Emergency SOS responsive web client designed for instant incident signaling. Utilizing the browser Geolocation API and Google Maps API, it computes your exact geographic details, saves active incidents, and bridges communication with trusted emergency contacts.

Emergency Procedures

  • Find Safety: Try to move to a safe, visible, and well-lit area.
  • Trigger SOS: Press and hold the primary SOS button. Let the countdown finish.
  • Contact Authorities: Use speed dial buttons to summon medical or safety services.
